Belize Hub — checkout / check-in field model

A local hub becomes the master record for the duration of a mission trip. Tablets connect over a local network with no internet; the hub reconciles to the cloud once a day over the legally sanctioned connection at the hotel.

Cloud / checkout path (brick) Local field network (sage) Nightly reconcile (dashed)
Why a hub and not per-device sync: a clinic runs registration → nurse → doctor → pharmacy across different people on different tablets. The hub gives live cross-device visibility during the day; without it, the doctor can't see the nurse's vitals until the nightly sync. The only actual sync is one writer (the hub), one direction, once a day, at rest — roughly a fifth of the complexity of bidirectional per-device sync. The government mandate pushed the design toward the architecture the workflow needed anyway.
