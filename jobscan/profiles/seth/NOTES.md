# Seth: the hunting map

Ranked. Campus beats off-campus for schedule mercy, safety, and resume value.

## On campus (best)
1. ERAU Student Employment portal + Handshake (log in with student account).
   Federal work-study and non-work-study student jobs both.
2. Tutoring / Learning Support: with 96-98% PLTW scores and documented peer
   tutoring, walk in and apply for math/physics/engineering tutor. This is the
   single best fit on his resume.
3. College of Engineering lab aide, machine shop, MakerLab: CAD/SolidWorks and
   FIRST Robotics prototyping experience is exactly what these want.
4. Library, campus rec, front desk seats: quiet-hour homework potential.

## Off campus, close (good)
5. Buc-ee's Daytona (LPGA Blvd): famously pays $16-21/hr for the area. Demanding
   pace, zero-phone culture. Best raw dollars within 10 minutes of campus.
6. Publix: student-friendly scheduling, real tuition reimbursement program.
7. Target, Ace Hardware, bike shops: standard, fine, safe.

## Rules baked into this profile
- Remote listings are auto-rejected (scam surface). If a legit remote campus job
  appears, paste it manually and it still gets the scam gate.
- Anything with pay-to-start, crypto, daily-cash, or Telegram language is
  auto-rejected with the reason logged in rejected.md.
