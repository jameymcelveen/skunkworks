# Slater: the market map and the money

## What the skills are worth (searched July 2026)
- Data center critical facilities technician: $60-90K to start, $90-130K as a
  critical facilities engineer. AWS, Google, Microsoft, Meta, Equinix, Digital
  Realty hire veterans aggressively: sign-on bonuses $5-15K, relocation, and
  paid cross-training are common. Shipboard redundant-power experience (UPS,
  generators, transfer switches, distribution) maps one-to-one.
- General EM-to-civilian listings: median ~$27-29/hr; nuclear-adjacent
  electrician roles $32-52/hr ($65-108K typical band).
- Industrial/plant electrician: $55K entry, $80-110K with experience.
- IBEW veteran direct-entry: Navy hours credit toward journeyman licensure
  (credit varies by state); among the best total-comp paths in the trades.
